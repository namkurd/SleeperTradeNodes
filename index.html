Every player value is normalized to a 0–100 scale within its own season, where 100 = the single most valuable player charted that season — this keeps seasons comparable even though the underlying value source changes over time. 2021–2022 values come from CBS Sports' single-QB redraft trade chart; 2023–2025 use CBS's 2QB/superflex chart (this league went superflex in 2023); both are matched to the week closest to each trade, using the nearest earlier chart when that exact week wasn't published. Starting in 2026, values come from a live 12-team, PPR, 2QB redraft trade-value feed instead of CBS. Team defenses and a handful of players never listed on a chart that season get a small flat placeholder (2–3 on the normalized scale) rather than a real market value. Trades where one side gave nothing (a player-for-FAAB trade, since FAAB isn't captured as an asset) are excluded entirely.
